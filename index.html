Backup & Restore

Court94 saves your teams and games only on this device. Download a backup regularly and keep it somewhere safe, such as the Files app, iCloud Drive, or email.

Tip: On iPad, add Court94 to your Home Screen (Share → Add to Home Screen). Safari may clear saved website data for sites that haven't been used for several days, so keep downloading backups regularly.
